Core Capabilities

💼

Financial Asset Registry

Consolidate bank accounts, demat folios, insurance policies, safe lockers, and liabilities with field-level masking and nominee status.

⏱️

Emergency Playbooks

Step-by-step action plans split into First 24 Hours, First 7 Days, and First 30 Days so family members know exactly who to call and what to file.

🔒

Zero Cloud Exposure

All records are stored strictly on the local device inside an isolated Room SQLite database. No third-party analytics or external telemetry leaks.

How to Install on Android

1

Download the APK file

Tap the Download APK button on your Android device's browser.

2

Allow "Install Unknown Apps"

When prompted by Android or Chrome, toggle "Allow from this source" in Settings so the package installer can proceed.

3

Open and Run

Tap the notification or find app-debug.apk in your Downloads folder to complete setup.
